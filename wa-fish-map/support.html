WA Fish Map — Support

Last updated: 2026-09-16

WA Fish Map is a Washington State fishing map of boat launches, piers, lakes and rivers with editorial seasonal timing.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or data corrections: danieldecena@icloud.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and the site name if a pin looks wrong.

Rules and seasons

The App shows timing guidance, not regulations. For seasons, closures and limits, use the official Fish Washington materials and wdfw.wa.gov. The App is not affiliated with or endorsed by WDFW.

Privacy

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. See the privacy policy.
